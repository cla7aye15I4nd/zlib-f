# Design history and references

These pages preserve completed design experiments and external sources. They explain why the
project chose its representation and which earlier arguments informed it. They are not current
acceptance evidence. [Overview](../overview.md) is the sole inventory of current work.

| Page | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Object-heap pilot](pilot.md) | What was tried, what was measured, why the heap was chosen and what the experiment omitted |
| [Reference material](references.md) | Pinned proof source, adapted ideas and limits of reuse |

Use the [design guide](../design/README.md) for the present architecture, the
[migration records](../migration/README.md) for argument-by-argument adaptation, and the
[checking guide](../checking/README.md) for acceptance. Historical compilation times, line counts
and reported tests are retained as measurements from those records. They were not rerun by the
documentation rewrite and do not establish CI success on a current commit.

[Legacy pilot link](../pilot.md) remains available for source comments that cite the original location.
